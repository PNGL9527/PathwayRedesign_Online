# Legend provenance and credits

The 58 PNG icons used in version 0.2 are extracted **unchanged** from the “Legends” sheet of the author-supplied **Peng Lu - Pathway Redesign Tool Example.xlsx**. Original colors, printed identifiers, role labels and emotional expressions are retained. No substitute illustrations or platform emoji are used.

| Category | Count | Source content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Avatars | 18 | Nurses, doctors, safety unit, surgeon, radiologist, radiographer, lab technician, cardiologist, physiotherapists, pharmacist, cleaning staff, case managers, manager and operator. Original identifiers 01–18. |
| Encounters | 20 | Touch, touch device, talking, medical intervention/check. Each includes identifiers 01–04 and a blank-number variant. |
| Locations | 10 | Interior space/room and building, each with identifiers 01–04 and a blank-number variant. |
| Emotional marks | 10 | Original faces numbered 01–10, from strongly negative to strongly positive. |
